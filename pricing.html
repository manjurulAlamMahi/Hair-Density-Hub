F.A.Q

Questions patients ask us

How much does a hair transplant cost in Lucknow?

Cost depends mainly on the number of grafts and the technique (FUE vs Sapphire FUE). After your scalp analysis you'll get a written, all-inclusive quote. See the Pricing page for guide prices and the Graft Estimator for an indicative graft range.

What is included in the price?

Packages include the procedure, blood tests, medicines, the first wash, an aftercare kit and follow-up visits. Your written quote lists exactly what is covered.
