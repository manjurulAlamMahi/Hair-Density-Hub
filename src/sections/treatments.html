FUE Hair Transplant

Healthy follicles are moved one by one from the donor area to thinning zones. No linear scar, with a natural-looking hairline.

Sapphire FUE

Sapphire-tipped blades make finer channels, which can mean denser packing, smaller wounds and quicker healing.

Beard & Eyebrow Transplant

Fill patchy beards and shape fuller brows using your own hair, angled to match natural growth direction.

Female Hair Loss

Diffuse thinning, widening parting or post-pregnancy shedding, diagnosed properly and treated medically or surgically.

PRP & GFC Therapy

Growth factors from your own blood, injected into the scalp to strengthen weak follicles and slow hair fall.

Corrective / Repair Transplant

Redesign of unnatural hairlines, pluggy grafts or low-density results from earlier procedures.

Laser Hair Reduction

Anti-Ageing, HIFU & Fillers
