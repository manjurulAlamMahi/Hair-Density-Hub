Your journey

From first message to final result

  1. 01

    Quick booking

    Book online in under a minute, or send a WhatsApp with photos of your scalp.

  2. 02

    Scalp analysis

    The doctor checks the cause of hair loss, your donor density and whether surgery is right for you.

  3. 03

    Hairline design

    Your hairline is drawn to suit your face shape and age, and you approve it before anything begins.

  4. 04

    Procedure day

    FUE / Sapphire FUE under local anaesthesia. Most patients watch TV or nap, and go home the same day.

  5. 05

    Aftercare

    Wash and medication plan, follow-up visits, and PRP sessions where advised to support growth.

  6. 06

    New hair

    Growth starts around month 3–4 and the full result appears by 10–12 months.

Week 1–2Scabs fall off, back to work in 2–3 days
Month 1–2Shock-loss phase. Transplanted hairs shed (normal)
Month 3–6New growth starts, hairline fills in
Month 10–12Final density & texture
