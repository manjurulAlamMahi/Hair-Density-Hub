FUE or Sapphire FUE: which one is right for you?

Both use the same extraction method. The difference is in how the channels are opened, and when that matters.

Patients often ask whether they should choose classic FUE or Sapphire FUE. The short answer: they are the same procedure with one step done differently.

What is the same

In both techniques, follicular units are removed one at a time from the donor area at the back and sides of the head. There is no strip and no stitched scar. The hairline design, anaesthesia and aftercare are identical.

What is different

Before the grafts are placed, small channels are opened in the thinning area. In classic FUE these are made with fine steel blades or needles. In Sapphire FUE they are made with blades cut from sapphire crystal, which stay very sharp and smooth.

When Sapphire FUE can help

  • When you want maximum density in the hairline, because the smaller channels allow grafts to sit closer together.
  • In large sessions, where hundreds of channels are opened and precision adds up.
  • For patients who want to keep redness and crusting to a minimum in the first week.

What matters more than the blade

The planning, the angle of each channel and how carefully the grafts are handled have a bigger effect on the result than the blade material. Ask to see results from the team, not just the technique name.

At your consultation the doctor will recommend one or the other based on your hair, your goals and your budget.
